1 · What the product is

Positioning

Explicitly not a job board, LMS, or professional network. A community ecosystem for Networking / Cloud / DevOps / Linux / Security / SRE / Platform / Automation professionals.

Core loop

Join → learn & practise → contribute → skill verified by peer/mentor → reputation & portfolio grow → mentorship, speaking, or a Community HR referral → mentor again.

The one artifact

A continuously evolving verified portfolio replaces the résumé. Trust Score, badges and professional levels are its currency.

“A resume tells you what someone claims. A community tells you what someone has proven.”

01_PROJECT_VISION.md — Core Belief

Five ordered pillars govern every downstream decision: Community First · Learning Before Hiring · Trust Before Resume · Contribution Before Reputation · Community Driven, Industry Connected. The order is load-bearing — reversing it is a governance escalation. Design consequence: learning and contribution surfaces outrank hiring surfaces in every hierarchy.

Three IA rules read as hard design constraints: anything findable in ≤3 steps; sensitive fields (private details, salary targets, audit trails) role-gated; and bias mitigation — sourcing search must hide identifying candidate fields, which means the sourcing engine's default card is badges-and-evidence, not name-and-photo.

3 · The only existing UI in the folder

visual_template/ is a four-tab business walkthrough — Personas, Service Blueprints, Capability Map, GTM & Pricing — driven by app.js. It is a documentation explorer, not the product. It contains no member, mentor, or company screen.

Its visual language: dark #0B0813 ground, translucent glass panels, Outfit headings over Inter body, gradient-clipped headline text, ambient purple/pink radial glow, cyan accent.

Nocturne — the bound design system — is the same instinct, disciplined: one dark ground, one blurple accent used as line and glow rather than flood, Inter throughout at weight 500, 0.7× density, outlined primary buttons, rules that fade at their ends. I'm treating Nocturne as the source of truth and the template as evidence of intended mood only.
