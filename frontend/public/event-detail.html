About this event

Everyone's Terraform is fine at twenty modules. At two hundred, the problems stop being about syntax and start being about people: who owns what, what a plan means when four teams touch the same state, and why the module you wrote to help everyone became the thing nobody can change.

Three engineers talk about the specific point at which their setup stopped working, and what they'd do differently. Then an open floor — bring the state file that's bothering you.

No prior Terraform experience assumed for the talks, though the open floor gets technical. Food from 18:30, talks from 19:00.
