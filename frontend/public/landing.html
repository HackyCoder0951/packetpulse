How it works

Four steps, and the third one is a person

No automated scoring, no quizzes. Trust comes from people who have seen your work putting their own name next to it.

Build a profile

Work history and real projects — link GitHub and it pulls the repos you choose to show.

Get endorsed

Ask people who have actually worked with you. They can decline, and often should.

Get verified

At five endorsements a chapter admin reviews the evidence. Roughly one in four is sent back.

Get placed

Recruiters search verified skills and request an introduction. You decide whether to reply.

If you're starting out

You have no commercial experience and every junior role wants two years of it. One endorsed skill from someone credible is worth more than a certificate nobody checks.

Students & freshers
If you're already working

Your best work is invisible outside your team. Verified skills travel with you, and recruiters who find you here already know what you can do.

Engineers
If you're hiring

Search by what people have proven, not what they've claimed. Every verified skill shows its endorsers and the admin who approved it — read it yourself.
