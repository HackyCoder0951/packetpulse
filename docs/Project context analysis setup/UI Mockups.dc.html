How it works

One loop, and it comes back around

Everyone enters as a learner — regardless of seniority. The people verifying your work this year were verified themselves two years ago.

Learn

Structured paths per domain, built and owned by mentors — not bought in.

Practise

Guided exercises, then unguided tasks in a real sandbox. Challenges quarterly.

Contribute

Answer questions, run sessions, volunteer at your chapter. All of it counts.

Get verified

Peers review first, a mentor decides. Never granted on course completion alone.

Give it back

Mentor, speak, lead a chapter — or be referred into a role you didn't apply for.

Domains

Networking first. The rest of the stack follows.

We start where the community already is. Each new domain opens as its own chapter only once there are mentors qualified to verify in it — trust standards travel, content does not get diluted.
