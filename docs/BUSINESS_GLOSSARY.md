# Business Glossary

> Canonical, standalone glossary for the Community Talent Ecosystem Platform Business Documentation Suite. For document-embedded definitions, see `18_APPENDIX.md`.

---

## A

**Achievement** — A recognized milestone of sustained or notable contribution (see `11_COMMUNITY_REPUTATION_SYSTEM.md`).

**Active Alumni** — A member who has moved into full-time employment but continues occasional community contribution (see `03_MEMBER_LIFECYCLE.md`).

**Admin (Community Admin)** — The role responsible for operational oversight of a chapter or functional area (see `04_COMMUNITY_OPERATIONS.md`).

**Alumni** — The lifecycle stage representing members who have transitioned into employment or reduced active participation while retaining community standing.

---

## B

**Badge** — A visible symbol of a verified skill, contribution, milestone, or leadership role (see `10_VERIFICATION_MODEL.md`, `11_COMMUNITY_REPUTATION_SYSTEM.md`).

**Blind Sourcing** — A candidate sourcing methodology that hides identifying demographic data to minimize hiring bias (see `20_STAKEHOLDER_ANALYSIS.md`, `37_POLICY_MANUAL.md`).

**Bulk Hiring** — A structured process for handling multiple simultaneous hiring requirements from a company (see `05_HR_OPERATIONS.md`).

---

## C

**Capability Maturity Level** — A metric (1 to 5) indicating the operational definition, monitoring, and optimization of a business capability (see `23_BUSINESS_CAPABILITY_MAP.md`).

**Chapter** — A semi-autonomous, geographically or thematically defined community unit operating under shared governance principles (see `02_COMMUNITY_BUSINESS_MODEL.md`).

**Chapter Playbook** — The official playbook for launching, operating, growing, and auditing local community chapters (see `28_CHAPTER_PLAYBOOK.md`).

**Code of Conduct Enforcement** — The moderation process for handling warnings, timeouts, suspensions, and bans (see `30_MODERATOR_HANDBOOK.md`, `37_POLICY_MANUAL.md`).

**Community First** — The foundational philosophy prioritizing community health over commercial interest.

**Community HR** — The trusted, community-embedded function that curates and recommends verified talent to hiring partners (see `05_HR_OPERATIONS.md`).

**Contribution Before Reputation** — The philosophy that reputation must always follow demonstrated contribution.

---

## D

**Double-Blind Mentor Verification** — An expert-level verification process requiring independent review by two separate, unaffiliated mentors (see `21_BUSINESS_RULES.md`).

---

## E

**Ethics Committee** — The governance body responsible for reviewing conduct and conflict-of-interest matters (see `13_COMMUNITY_GOVERNANCE.md`).

---

## G

**Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy** — The market entry and growth plan for member acquisition, chapter expansion, and B2B partnerships (see `33_GO_TO_MARKET_STRATEGY.md`).

**Governance Committee** — The top-level, community-linked policy-setting body (see `13_COMMUNITY_GOVERNANCE.md`).

---

## H

**Hiring Request** — A formal submission by a company describing an open role's requirements (see `06_COMPANY_PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md`).

---

## L

**Learning Before Hiring** — The philosophy that growth is the entry point to the ecosystem, not a hiring funnel.

**Learning Path** — A structured, domain-specific curriculum guiding member growth (see `08_LEARNING_ECOSYSTEM.md`).

---

## M

**Mentor** — A senior, trusted member who guides other members and participates in skill verification (see `03_MEMBER_LIFECYCLE.md`, `10_VERIFICATION_MODEL.md`).

**Mentor Council** — The governance body overseeing mentorship standards and mentor promotion decisions (see `13_COMMUNITY_GOVERNANCE.md`).

**Moderator** — A community role responsible for day-to-day content and conduct moderation (see `04_COMMUNITY_OPERATIONS.md`).

---

## N

**Non-Influence Clause** — A contractual safeguard preventing sponsors or companies from affecting verification, reputation, or hiring outcomes (see `07_SPONSORSHIP_MODEL.md`, `06_COMPANY_PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md`).

---

## P

**Placement** — The successful outcome of a candidate being hired through the Community HR process (see `05_HR_OPERATIONS.md`).

**Portfolio** — A member's continuously evolving professional identity record within the ecosystem (see `01_PROJECT_VISION.md`).

**Practice Roadmap** — A structured sequence of exercises and challenges building a member toward verification-readiness (see `09_LAB_AND_PRACTICE_MODEL.md`).

**Professional Level** — A tiered classification (e.g., Emerging, Practitioner, Proficient, Expert, Distinguished) reflecting a member's verified standing (see `10_VERIFICATION_MODEL.md`).

---

## R

**RACI Matrix** — A grid mapping process activities to roles showing who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed (see `27_RACI_MATRIX.md`).

**Release Phase** — A planned block of product feature rollouts aligned with business maturity targets (see `39_RELEASE_PLANNING.md`).

**Reputation** — The visible, structured record of a member's community contribution over time (see `11_COMMUNITY_REPUTATION_SYSTEM.md`).

**Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)** — A grid mapping vision objectives to rules, capabilities, processes, user stories, and backlog features (see `40_TRACEABILITY_MATRIX.md`).

**Retention (Placement)** — The tracked, sustained success of a placement over time (see `05_HR_OPERATIONS.md`).

---

## S

**Service Blueprint** — An operational diagram aligning member actions, frontstage touches, backstage operations, and support processes (see `25_SERVICE_BLUEPRINT.md`).

**Sponsor** — An organization funding community initiatives (events, scholarships, programs) without influencing merit-based outcomes (see `07_SPONSORSHIP_MODEL.md`).

---

## T

**Talent Pool** — A domain-organized collection of verified members visible to hiring partners (see `06_COMPANY_PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md`).

**Training Partner** — An organization contributing learning content and distribution to the community (see `02_COMMUNITY_BUSINESS_MODEL.md`).

**Trust Before Resume** — The philosophy that verified, community-endorsed proof outranks self-declared claims.

**Trust Score** — A composite business-level indicator of a member's verified standing and community credibility (see `10_VERIFICATION_MODEL.md`).

---

## V

**Verification** — The community process of converting a claimed skill into a peer/mentor-endorsed, trusted fact (see `10_VERIFICATION_MODEL.md`).

**Verification Council** — The escalation-only governance body handling disputed verification decisions (see `10_VERIFICATION_MODEL.md`, `13_COMMUNITY_GOVERNANCE.md`).

**Verified Member** — A member holding at least one community-verified skill.

**Volunteer** — A member contributing operational support to the community (content, events, member support) (see `04_COMMUNITY_OPERATIONS.md`).

---

**See also:** `18_APPENDIX.md` for embedded definitions and abbreviations table.
